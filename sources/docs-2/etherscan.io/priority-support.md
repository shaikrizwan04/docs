# Need Fast, Expert Assistance?

Etherscan provides free Standard support for all users, but due to the high volume of daily requests, response times via standard channels may take up to 7-10 business days

If your request is time-sensitive or requires faster handling, or requests that require technical expertise, manual review, or special attention, you may opt for our Priority Support service — a paid, expedited option that guarantees a response within 48 business hours.

## What We Offer

Our priority support covers the following

![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/token-update-light.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/token-update-dim-blue.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/token-update-dark.svg?v=26.9.2.3)

### Token Update Requests

We offer assistance with expedited service in:

- Updating token details (project website, logo, social links)
- Refreshing metadata, team info, or project links
- Resolving token listing display issues

Requirements: Ensure that the token information is submitted. Check our token update guidelines [here](https://info.etherscan.com/how-to-update-token-info/).

![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/smart-contract-light.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/smart-contract-dim-blue.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/smart-contract-dark.svg?v=26.9.2.3)

### Smart Contract Verification Support

Having trouble verifying your smart contract? We provide expedited support on:

- Debugging of compiler settings, constructor arguments and metadata mismatches
- Manual verification requests and smart contract specific troubleshootings

![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/others-light.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/others-dim-blue.svg?v=26.9.2.3) ![](https://etherscan.io/assets/svg/priority-support/others-dark.svg?v=26.9.2.3)
