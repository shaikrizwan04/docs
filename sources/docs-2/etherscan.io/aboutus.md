## Our History

Built and launched in 2015 by **Matthew Tan** (CEO & Founder), Etherscan is one of the earliest and longest-running independent projects built around Ethereum and its community, with the mission of providing equitable access to blockchain data.

- 2015

 August

 Launched Etherscan, a block explorer for Ethereum

 September

 Launched explorer [API services](https://etherscan.io/api/pricing) for Ethereum

- 2016

 April

 Launched smart contract [verification](https://etherscan.io/verifyContract)

 July

 Supported the ERC-20 token standard

 August

 Joined the Boost VC startup accelerator

- 2017

 January

 Moved into an office (where we still are today!)

 May

 Supported Ethereum Name Service (ENS) lookups

 December

 Displayed images and metadata for CryptoKitties, one of the first NFTs

- 2018

 January

 Launched [verified signatures](https://etherscan.io/verifiedSignatures)

 June

 Launched the Etherscan [Gas Tracker](https://etherscan.io/gastracker)

 October

 Launched [labels](https://etherscan.io/labelcloud), providing key context to onchain addresses

- 2019

 February

 Refreshed Etherscan's design

- 2020

 January

 Published the first edition of Etherscan's [newsletter](https://info.etherscan.com/newsletters/)

 June

 Launched Blockscan, a web3 search engine

 July

 Launched [BeaconScan](https://beaconscan.com/), an explorer for Ethereum's Beacon Chain

 Launched and created [Explorer as a Service (EaaS)](https://etherscan.io/explorer-as-a-service-eaas), a full-featured block explorer service

- 2021

 May

 Etherscan became one of the top 1,000 websites in the world by traffic

 July

 Added multichain transaction search on Blockscan

- 2022

 January

 EaaS services its 10th client

 Launched [Blockscan Chat](https://chat.blockscan.com/), an encrypted, wallet-to-wallet messaging app

 September

 Supported the Merge, Ethereum's switch to Proof of Stake

 November

 Launched the Etherscan Chrome [extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/etherscan/joeoaocmnapjmkhjndfflecmdaldkpbn)

- 2023

 April

 Launched the Etherscan [Metadata API](https://docs.etherscan.io/metadata/introduction)

 July

 EaaS services its 20th client

 August

 Launched the Blockscan Chat mobile app on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blockscan/id6451138131) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.etherscan.blockscan&hl=en&pli=1)

- 2024

 January

 Acquired Solscan, a block explorer for Solana

 July

 Launched the Blockscan [Multichain Explorer](https://blockscan.com/), supporting 30+ blockchains in one website

 October

 EaaS services its 30th client

 Launched Etherscan API V2, supporting 50+ blockchains with one API key
