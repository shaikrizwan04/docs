# Etherscan Brand Assets & Guidelines

Thank you for your support and loving our work. Below, we have provided a few guidelines for using our brand assets.

---

## Our Brand Name

"Etherscan" is one word and spelled with a capital E.

## Branding Guidelines

You may use the Etherscan name and logo in your website or app as long as you adhere to the Etherscan branding guidelines.

- Do not alter the Etherscan logo. The logo is to be used as provided.
- Do not combine Etherscan logo with other images without consent.
- Do not use the Etherscan brand in any way that implies a relationship, affiliation, or endorsement. For example, "we partner with Etherscan."
- Do reference Etherscan as follows:
 - "Powered by Etherscan"
 - "Data provided by Etherscan"
 - "Source: Etherscan"
- Do not use the Etherscan brand in conjunction with any illegal and unlawful activity, promotion, and product.
