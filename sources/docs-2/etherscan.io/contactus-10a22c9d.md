# Etherscan Phishing Form

The purpose of this form is to collect reports of phishing/scam attempts and incidents that are causing users to lose funds. The information collected in this form will help us to "Tag" or "Label" the reported phishing/scam addresses on Etherscan.io to warn and inform users that the addresses are involved in unethical activities (high risk/fake). As a Block Explorer service, we can only provide and display information on transactions that occur on the Ethereum Block Explorer. We are unable to provide or process any refunds of scammed funds. Please include the proof of scam otherwise the report is treated as false positive. PS : Do not send a screenshot from https://etherscan.io/
